### The rules that don't bend

Covers the hard rules no style or profile overrides, and how the person's taste beats the rest.

A hard rule is about correctness or honesty, not taste. Break one and the video is broken, or
says something that isn't true, whatever its style: a clipped word, a flash of the wrong take, a
picture passed off as real, a check that never happened. No competent editor would do the
opposite on purpose. Each one is explained where it's marked `(hard rule)` in its own file.

Honest about the material:

- Never sign up, paste a key, or send footage to a service that isn't set up. [read.md](read.md)
- A cut the person made is final: don't move it or change its length. [read.md](read.md)
- A cold open is the video's own moment, true to what was meant in context. [shape.md](shape.md)
- Never show a generated picture as evidence, as real, or with unsaid numbers. [broll.md](broll.md)
- Show the real chart, post or page: never redrawn or swapped for a look-alike. [broll.md](broll.md)
- A screen filmed again is the real page, its numbers read from the recording. [broll.md](broll.md)
- Use accounts read-only, and keep keys and tokens out of anything you save. [broll.md](broll.md)
- Keep where every clip came from and its license, and say which are generated. [broll.md](broll.md)
- Captions are exactly what was said, timed to the words. [text.md](text.md)
- Every name, number and count on screen has a source you can point to. [text.md](text.md)
- Every track has a clear license for where the video is going. [sound.md](sound.md)
- Copy how other videos are edited, never their footage, music or words. [notes.md](notes.md)

Nothing broken on screen or in the sound:

- Cut in silence between phrases, never inside a word. [cut.md](cut.md)
- Place each cut by the sound, not the transcript's times, and check both sides. [cut.md](cut.md)
- A cut is the exact frame the picture changes; what starts or ends there is on it. [cut.md](cut.md)
- Never use a time read on one clock (source, cut, final) on another. [transcript.md](transcript.md)
- Never fade the last word. [shape.md](shape.md)
- A highlight is gone, fade included, before its shot changes. [broll.md](broll.md)
- Never cover a mouth, a talking face, or what the viewer needs to read. [text.md](text.md)
- A shade or gradient under text never stays after its words. [text.md](text.md)
- Text behind a person needs their cutout on every frame it shows. [text.md](text.md)
- Fade every hard audio cut, so joins don't click. [sound.md](sound.md)

Honest about your own work:

- Measure the loudness of the exported file; never state one you didn't. [sound.md](sound.md)
- If you can't hear it, say so; never say something was checked when it wasn't. [check.md](check.md)
- Hand over with what you changed and assumed, and how each claim was established: measured,
  looked at, or not checked. [check.md](check.md)

#### Everything else is a default

The rest of this guide is the general opinion that suits most people: pause and shot lengths,
caption sizes, music levels, how often B-roll appears, zooms, how things arrive and leave. A
person's own taste beats it. When two sources disagree, the first in this list wins:

1. the person's words in this conversation;
2. the project's `editing.md`;
3. their personal `~/.editing-videos/editing.md`;
4. this guide's defaults.

None of the first three overrides a hard rule. When a profile or a request conflicts with one,
don't quietly follow either: say what conflicts and why it matters, and ask.

When there is no profile, use the defaults, and at the end say which ones mattered (the pauses
you kept, the music level, the caption size), so the person can correct them.

#### Numbers say where they come from

Every judgment number in this guide carries one tag, after it or at the end of its sentence; a
line under a table tags the table.

- `(standard)`: a published platform or broadcast standard, named once in its file. It changes
  only when the destination's standard does.
- `(measured)`: measured on real edits, named once in its file. It describes those edits.
- `(common)`: several independent published editing guides agree; no single source.
- `(house)`: this guide's own starting point.

A `(house)` or `(common)` number is the first to give way to the person's profile. No invented
numbers: if nothing supports a figure, it is `(house)` and says so.
